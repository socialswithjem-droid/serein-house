# 1. BRAND IDENTITY

Brand Name:

SEREIN HOUSE

Tagline:

Fragrance for slower living.

Brand description:

Serein House is an independent fragrance and body-care house inspired by the quiet parts of everyday life: rain against an open window, freshly washed sheets, warm skin after a shower, late-afternoon sunlight, and rooms that still carry traces of the people who were there.

The collection includes personal fragrance, body care, candles, room fragrance, and curated ritual sets.

Rather than creating loud statement scents, Serein House creates intimate compositions designed to live close to the skin and become part of everyday routines.

Brand personality:

• sensory  
• editorial  
• intimate  
• sophisticated  
• warm  
• contemporary  
• artistic  
• quietly confident  
• premium but approachable

Target customer:

Design-conscious customers approximately 22–40 years old who appreciate fragrance, skincare, interiors, lifestyle products, beautiful packaging, slow living, and thoughtful design.

OUR APPROACH

We build fragrances around atmosphere rather than spectacle.

MADE TO LIVE WITH

# 34. FAQ PAGE

Heading:

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED.

Create accordion categories.

ORDERS

Can I change my order after placing it?

Orders begin processing quickly. Contact us as soon as possible and we'll do our best to help before the order ships.

Can I cancel an order?

Orders that have not entered fulfillment may be cancelled. Contact customer care with your order number.

---

SHIPPING

When will my order ship?

Demo copy:

Orders typically leave our studio within 1–3 business days.

How much is shipping?

Complimentary standard shipping is available on orders over $75. Shipping costs for smaller orders are calculated at checkout.

---

RETURNS

Can I return my order?

Unopened and unused products may be returned within 30 days of delivery.

For hygiene reasons, opened body-care products cannot be returned.

---

FRAGRANCE

How should I choose a fragrance?

Start with the Discovery Set if you're unsure. Fragrance develops differently on every person, so we recommend wearing each sample for several hours before deciding.

How long does fragrance last?

Longevity depends on skin, environment, application, and composition. Eau de parfum generally remains noticeable for several hours.

---

PRODUCTS

Are your products tested on animals?

Serein House does not test finished products on animals.

Where can I find ingredients?

Full ingredient information appears on each relevant product page and product packaging.
